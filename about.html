About
EventEase brings your celebrations to life with beautiful designs, affordable pricing, and stress-free planning. We organise 10+ event types, each with multiple decoration themes and attractive discounts — 15% off on the first booking, 20% off on the third, and 25% off after five bookings. Our aim is to give you a premium event experience at a reasonable cost.

For food, we offer three flexible choices: Veg, Non-Veg, and Both. Each option includes a wide range of dishes, allowing you to pick 15, 20, 25, or 30+ items, all calculated using a per-plate cost system for better affordability. With plenty of tasty options, our menu is designed to suit every event and every guest.

Apart from the themes shown on our website, you can upload your own decoration idea in PDF format, and we will recreate it perfectly. Every booking includes a free welcome board gift on the event day. And if you feel something is missing, you can suggest it through the Queries section — we always try our best to improve your event experience.
